The Magic of Physical Modeling

Here at RMI, we are currently creating and testing a collection of synths and effects. Our philosophy: make instruments that are human. And fun. If you’re a rocket scientist, maybe you’ll enjoy them too.

We’re fans of physically modeled sounds. What does that mean? Nothing important, except that the sounds and effects are realistic and wildly flexible.

We’re in alpha. Stay tuned.
