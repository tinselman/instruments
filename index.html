RM Instruments was founded by Robyn Miller, the co-founder of Cyan Worlds and co-creator and composer of Myst and Riven. After years of scoring games and films, he found himself searching for production tools that didn’t exist. That search became the foundation of RM Instruments. Our philosophy: music software shouldn’t feel like homework. It should speak the language of the musician.

Rock Out and Plugin

Here at Robyn Miller Instruments, we’ve been hard at work building a collection of synths and effects plugins. And we’re almost complete! As composers, these are the tools we want to use: powerful, easy-to-use, and wildly flexible.
